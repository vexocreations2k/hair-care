# RootCare: hair performance check from a face photo

A face photo shows the front of the hair only. It cannot judge the crown or the back of the head.

## Checks the app makes
| Check | What the app looks at | Result shown |
|---|---|---|
| Hairline shape | Position and symmetry of hairline and temples | Stable, receding at temples, or M-shape |
| Hairline change | Today's photo against your first photo | Moved back, same, or improved |
| Front density | How much scalp shows at the front | Dense, medium, or thin |
| Hair thickness | Strand width against earlier photos | Same, finer, or thicker |
| Parting width | How wide the parting looks | Narrow, normal, or wide |
| Scalp condition | Visible flakes, redness, oiliness | Clear, mild, or needs care |
| Photo quality | Light, angle, cap or product in the way | Good, or retake the photo |

## Performance result
Score out of 100 for the front of the hair (hairline, density, thickness, scalp), a status (Strong, Stable, Needs attention, See a doctor) and the change since the last photo. Take a monthly photo in the same light and angle.

## Limits
- Cannot see thinning at the crown.
- Cannot tell the cause of hair loss.
- Not a diagnosis. Bald patches, sudden shedding, pain or sores: see a doctor.

## Suggested photo set
Front face, top of head looking down, and the parting.

## Privacy
Ask permission before saving photos, store them on the device if possible, and let the user delete them.
