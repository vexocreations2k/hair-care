Treatments we use

Your doctor picks from these after the scalp check. Many people combine two.

Topical minoxidil

Applied daily to extend the growth phase. Works best on early thinning at the crown.

Oral medication

Prescription options that reduce hormone-driven hair loss. Needs a doctor's review first.

PRP therapy

Platelet-rich plasma from your own blood, injected into the scalp every 4 to 6 weeks at first.

Low-level laser

A home cap used every other day. A gentle option that pairs well with other treatments.

Nutrition and scalp care

Blood tests for iron, vitamin D and thyroid, plus a scalp routine for dandruff and oil.

Questions

Does hair growth treatment work for everyone?

No. Results depend on the cause. Hair loss from genetics, stress, hormones or low iron each need a different approach, which is why we test first.

Why does my hair shed more at the start?

Treatment pushes resting hairs out so new ones can grow. This usually lasts a few weeks and then eases.

Is PRP painful?

You feel small pricks. A numbing cream is used and most people return to normal activity the same day.

Do I have to continue treatment forever?

Hair loss from genetics tends to return if treatment stops. Your doctor will talk through a lighter maintenance plan once you reach your goal.
